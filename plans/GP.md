# ⏰ TIME MANAGEMENT RULE

Use this simple rule throughout the trip:

### 🟢 On Schedule
Continue with the planned itinerary.

### 🟡 30–45 Minutes Behind
Shorten the current stop.

### 🟠 1 Hour+ Behind
Skip the **optional** attraction.

### 🔴 Significantly Behind / Driver Tired
Skip sightseeing and go directly to the hotel.

**Never compensate for lost time by speeding.**

# 🏁 Trip Goal

The goal is **not to visit the maximum number of places**.

The goal is:

> **Drive safely → enjoy the Konkan coast → avoid rushing → reach each hotel comfortably → have enough energy to enjoy the next day.**
