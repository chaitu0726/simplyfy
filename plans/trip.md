# 🏖️ 4-Day / 3-Night Konkan Road Trip
## Mandwa → Guhagar → Velneshwar → Hedvi → Anjanvel → Harihareshwar → Diveagar → Mandwa

> **Trip type:** Beginner-friendly coastal road trip  
> **Starting point:** Mandwa, Maharashtra  
> **Duration:** 4 Days / 3 Nights  
> **Target distance:** ~700–800 km depending on exact roads and detours  
> **Style:** Beaches + coastal roads + lighthouse + fort + temples + relaxed sightseeing

---

## Overnight Plan

| Night | Stay | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Night 1 | **Guhagar** | Reach after the long first drive and enjoy the beach |
| Night 2 | **Guhagar** | Avoid changing hotels; explore Velneshwar, Hedvi & Anjanvel |
| Night 3 | **Diveagar** | Convenient after visiting Harihareshwar & Shrivardhan |

> **Why 2 nights in Guhagar?**

# 2. ⭐ Hedvi + Baman Ghal

### Priority: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — MUST DO

**Velneshwar → Hedvi: ~5–10 km**

### Suggested time

**10:15–12:00**

### Visit

- **Hedvi Dashabhuja Ganpati Temple**
- **Baman Ghal**

### Baman Ghal

Baman Ghal is a natural rocky coastal gorge where seawater enters the narrow rock formation.

### ⚠️ Safety

- Check sea/tide conditions.
- Do not climb onto wet or slippery rocks.
- Stay away from the edge during rough sea.
- Don't enter the gorge.

> Baman Ghal is a sightseeing/photo stop, not a place to take risks for photographs.

---

# 3. ⭐ Anjanvel Lighthouse

### Priority: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Hedvi → Anjanvel**

### Suggested time

**12:30–13:30**

### Important

# 4. ⭐ Gopalgad Fort

### Priority: ⭐⭐⭐⭐

### Suggested time

**13:30–14:30**

The attraction is mainly:

- Historic coastal fort
- Sea views
- Location
- Photography

### Janjira Fort
Excellent fort, but it requires a boat trip and can consume several hours.

Worth a separate trip; adding it makes this itinerary more rushed.

### Ganpatipule
Too far south for this particular 4-day loop without sacrificing the places you specifically wanted.

### Ratnagiri
Better combined with Ganpatipule/other southern Konkan destinations on another trip.

# 🚗 Beginner Road-Trip Tips

- Start early each day.
- Don't assume 100 km = 1 hour in Konkan.
- Keep at least **1–2 hours of buffer** each day.
- Avoid aggressive overtaking on narrow coastal roads.
- Fill fuel before entering longer coastal stretches.
- Download offline maps before leaving.
- Don't rely exclusively on mobile network around remote coastal stretches.
- Check weather and sea conditions before visiting rocky locations such as Baman Ghal.
- Don't drive after drinking.
- Keep Day 4 intentionally light so the final return isn't exhausting.
